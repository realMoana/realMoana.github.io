I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science at Florida International University, working on explainable and trustworthy AI. My research develops faithful explanation and evaluation methods for graph neural networks and time-series models, with recent work extending to LLM agents and efficient on-device AI. My work has appeared in TPAMI, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, AAAI, and KDD.

  • faithful explanation methods for graph neural networks
  • robust evaluation frameworks for explainability
  • time-series explanation, forecasting, and reasoning
  • reliable LLM agents and efficient on-device AI
